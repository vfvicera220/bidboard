Direction · Focus / one-thumb

A single-purpose personality: the price is a 72px hero, chrome shrinks to a thumbnail row, and every control lives in a rounded bottom sheet within thumb reach. The number counts up on each new bid and the Bid button pulses once when you're outbid.

FAST — from link to bid is one thumb movement, no scrolling. EASY TO USE — three big targets (−, +, Bid) and nothing else competing. RELIABLE — "Next valid ₱4,600" is always shown above the button, so the amount you tap is the amount you bid.
