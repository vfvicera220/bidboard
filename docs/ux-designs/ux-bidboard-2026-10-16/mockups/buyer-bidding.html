BidBoard · Buyer bidding

Ticker direction with softened corners on the Ledger palette: hairline grids, tabular figures, a price that ticks ▲ +100 on every bid (120ms hard colour flash, no easing), and one fixed action bar. Final tokens, type and microcopy come from DESIGN.md and EXPERIENCE.md; if anything here disagrees, those documents win.

Every frame shows one control in its keyboard-focus state (3px focus ring with a 2px gap) so the ring can be checked against each surface.
